## DEC-GPS-RET-20261109 — Amend G4: seven-day restricted historical retention

- **Stop live tracking immediately** when the trip ends or the driver's approved shift ends (whichever first). Invalidate current/live GPS read authorization immediately; no GPS collection out of shift or after terminal. The 30-minute collection cadence and at most 100 positions per batch remain unchanged.
- **Retain valid previously collected raw GPS for up to 7 days** after the authorized collection window has ended, solely for defined trip-related investigation/complaint resolution, **not** to continue employee monitoring. The historical store must be separately secured from the live-position service, with market/trip-specific access, authorized reviewer, explicit case/reason and immutable access audit. Do not treat Dispatcher live authorization G2 as permission to browse historical closed trips.
- **Delete stored location points at expiration** using scheduled, idempotent cleanup; ensure any caches, exports and backups comply with an approved erasure policy. Offline device queues may never keep or later upload data from an ended duty/trip; purge invalid pending uploads immediately. Do not delete POD, milestones, regulatory duty/rest evidence or immutable non-GPS audit as a side effect.
- **Privacy and rollout conditions:** Real-employee GPS history requires explicit approved purpose, documented lawful basis, necessity/proportionality, worker notices and per-driver acknowledgment evidence, access review, DPIA/legal/privacy sign-off and backup handling. Approval of seven-day product retention is **not** legal clearance to begin real-worker collection or unrestricted retrospective surveillance.
- **Incident preservation/exception:** Any request to preserve data beyond seven days needs separately approved, legally assessed case-specific preservation/hold procedures; there is **no automatic indefinite extension** under this decision.
- **Implementation is pending:** update D3 GPS services and M6 terminal hooks, retention config, user-facing privacy notice, authorization/data model and tests. The prior immediate raw deletion rule in G4 is superseded, **not** retroactively implemented. Do not auto-merge code PRs.
